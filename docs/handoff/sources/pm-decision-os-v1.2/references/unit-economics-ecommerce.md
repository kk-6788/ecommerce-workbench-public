# E-commerce Unit Economics

## Purpose

Determine whether a product becomes healthier as sales increase.

---

# 1. Contribution Margin

Use:

Net Revenue  
- Product Cost  
- Packaging  
- Inbound Freight  
- Fulfillment / Shipping  
- Platform Fees  
- Payment Fees  
- Advertising Cost  
- Expected Returns / Refunds  
- Defect / Damage Allowance  
- Customer Support Cost  
- Storage Cost  
- Promotion Cost  
= Contribution Margin

Where taxes or duties are material, include them.

---

# 2. Expected Return Cost

Do not wait until real returns happen.

Estimate:

Expected Return Cost  
= Return Probability × Average Loss Per Return

Average loss may include:

- reverse shipping
- non-resellable inventory
- refund handling
- replacement
- support labor
- platform penalties

---

# 3. Advertising

Separate:

## Organic Economics
Does the product work without paid acquisition?

## Paid Economics
Does it remain healthy after CAC / ad spend?

Useful metrics:

- CAC
- ROAS
- MER where applicable
- contribution margin after ads

High ROAS does not automatically mean high profit.

---

# 4. Cash Efficiency

Analyze:

- upfront inventory cost
- MOQ
- lead time
- payment terms
- inventory days
- payout delay
- replenishment time

A product with lower margin but fast cash recovery may be preferable during survival stage.

---

# 5. Scenarios

Use at least:

## Conservative
Lower conversion, higher CAC, higher return rate.

## Base
Reasonable expectation.

## Upside
Better but plausible performance.

A product that only works in the upside scenario is fragile.

---

# 6. Kill Logic

Potential kill triggers:

- negative contribution margin in base case
- unacceptable downside in conservative case
- margin highly dependent on unrealistically low CAC
- return rate destroys profit
- logistics cost is too sensitive to small changes
- promotion is required permanently

---

# 7. Output

Report:

- Selling price
- Net revenue
- Variable cost stack
- Contribution margin
- Contribution margin %
- Expected return cost
- CAC assumption
- Cash tied up
- Break-even CAC
- Break-even return rate
- Scenario sensitivity
