# Supply and Inventory Risk

## Purpose

Protect cash and operational stability.

---

# 1. Supplier Risk

Assess:

- supplier count
- backup suppliers
- MOQ
- lead time
- defect rate
- spec consistency
- packaging consistency
- communication reliability
- capacity
- price volatility
- dependency on one factory

---

# 2. Inventory Risk

Assess:

- unit cost
- MOQ cash exposure
- sell-through uncertainty
- seasonality
- obsolescence
- variant complexity
- storage burden
- replenishment delay
- liquidation value

---

# 3. Variant Risk

High variant counts can create hidden inventory fragmentation.

Examples:

- sizes
- colors
- breeds
- compatibility variants
- materials

A product with 10 variants may require much more working capital than one headline SKU suggests.

---

# 4. Inventory Decision

Prefer early-stage products with:

- low MOQ
- flexible replenishment
- low variant complexity
- stable supply
- small storage footprint
- salvageable inventory
- short lead time

---

# 5. Supply Kill Criteria

Potential kill:

- single supplier with no substitute
- high MOQ relative to test budget
- inconsistent quality
- long lead time with volatile demand
- fragile packaging
- high damage rate
- complex QC requirements beyond capability

---

# 6. Output

- MOQ
- Test order cash exposure
- Lead time
- Supplier redundancy
- Variant count
- Storage burden
- Defect risk
- Replenishment risk
- Inventory risk verdict
