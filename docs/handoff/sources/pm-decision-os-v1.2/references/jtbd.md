# Jobs To Be Done

## Objective

Understand why a user chooses a product in a specific situation.

Do not rely only on demographic personas.

Use:

> When [situation], I want to [motivation], so I can [expected outcome].

---

## JTBD Components

### Trigger
What event creates the need?

### Job
What progress is the user trying to make?

### Functional Need
What practical outcome is desired?

### Emotional Need
How does the user want to feel?

### Social Need
How does the user want to be perceived?

### Pain
What is difficult, risky, expensive, or frustrating?

### Alternative
What does the user use today?

### Barrier
What prevents switching or purchasing?

### Success
What does a successful outcome look like?

### Purchase Driver
What makes action urgent enough to pay?

---

## Interview Prompts

Prefer specific past behavior over hypothetical future intent.

Ask:

- Tell me about the last time this happened.
- What triggered it?
- What did you do first?
- What did you try?
- What was frustrating?
- What did it cost in money or time?
- What nearly stopped you from solving it?
- Why did you choose that solution?
- What would have made you switch?

Avoid overusing:

- Would you buy this?
- Do you like this idea?
- Would this feature be useful?

Stated intention is weaker than observed behavior.
